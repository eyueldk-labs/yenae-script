# Surface: catalog (home + script panel)

Mode: Operate. Audience: the owner and non-technical people they share it with. Ease of use first; the look must make it inviting, never busier.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A shelf of small tools you can read at a glance, like a rack of seed packets: the front says what it is, the back says how to use it. Refuses the grey admin grid with one blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Each script owns one saturated packet colour (ultramarine, leaf, tomato, plum, marigold, teal, poppy, slate; all ≥5:1 with white). Cards are packets: a colour front carrying a white icon, then a plain label with the name and one line of what it does. Ground is a cool paper white (#f5f6f2; dark #111311), cards white (dark #1a1d1a). Names and headings in Bricolage Grotesque (bundled); body in the system face. Primary actions in ink (#1c1f1b); inside a script's panel, its own packet colour.

STORY: People see what each tool does without opening it, notice anything that needs them from the stickers, click a packet, fill in the back, press Run.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the wordmark, search, New script, Settings. Alerts that need you as sentences with one button. Favourites, then other scripts and folders, as packet cards in a grid. The open script sits in a panel on the right whose header is the packet front at full width.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5 of 7 (seed-packet rack), assigned by roll. seed:e6cd1d442cb08a49 (surface), direction roll 50df…; signature move: status as a round sticker on the packet front, in words ("Failed", "Needs you", "Running").

GUARDRAILS: Controls stay standard (buttons, tabs, inputs, selects). No packet ornament beyond colour front, icon and sticker; no paper texture, no illustrations, no cream ground. Every status is words plus colour, never colour alone.
